qr
Generate QR codes in the terminal with ANSI VT-100 escape codes.
More information:
https://github.com/lincolnloop/python-qrcode/
.
Generate a QR code:
echo "{{data}}" | qr
Specify the error correction level (defaults to M):
echo "{{data}}" | qr --error-correction={{L|M|Q|H}}
