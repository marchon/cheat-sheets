sleep

 
Delay for a specified amount of time. More information: https://www.gnu.org/software/coreutils/sleep.
 
  • Delay in seconds:
     
    sleep {{seconds}}
     
  • Delay in minutes:
     
    sleep {{minutes}}m
     
  • Delay in hours:
     
    sleep {{hours}}h
